Three generations

Picked at first light, the way our grandmother did.

Kinmoor started with one family recipe book and a hillside garden. We still buy only from growers we know by name, and only the first two leaves and a bud.

Every batch is blended by hand, tasted, and sealed the same week, so the cup you brew tastes the way it did on the hill.
